**Workshop Guide: From Prompt to Publish -- AI Meets Website Creation**

**Audience**: 10 Participants\
**Facilitators**: 3--4 Instructors\
**Tools Needed**: Laptops with internet, ChatGPT (Pro preferred),
Lovable accounts, Projector, Printouts (Brand Guide, Persona Worksheet,
Prompt Cheat Sheet)

**🔷 Session 1 (9:00 AM -- 9:30 AM): Intro to AI: Light, Fun & Human**

**Objective**: Introduce AI in simple, non-technical terms and explain
its role in the day ahead.

**Format**: Interactive Lecture (Lead Instructor)

**Activities**:

-   **Warm Welcome**: Icebreaker prompt - \"What would you use AI for
    today if you had a magic assistant?\"

-   **What is AI?** -- Calculator analogy, simple breakdown of machine
    learning and generative AI.

-   **Who's Who**: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google
    (Gemini), Meta (LLaMA)

-   **Use Cases**: 5-minute brainstorm: \"Where do you see AI in the
    wild?\"

-   **Busting Myths**: AI isn't here to replace you; it's here to assist
    you.

**Outcome**: Understand what AI is and how it will help throughout the
day.

**🔷 Session 1.5 (9:30 AM -- 9:40 AM): ChatGPT Setup**

**Objective**: Ensure everyone is logged in and ready to work inside
ChatGPT.

**Activities**:

-   Create ChatGPT account (free or Pro)

-   Walkthrough of ChatGPT interface

-   Introduce \"Projects\" tab (for Pro users)

**Outcome**: All participants are logged into ChatGPT and ready to
build.

**🔷 Session 2 (9:40 AM -- 11:00 AM): Mastering ChatGPT Projects for
Website Creation**

**Objective**: Teach users to organize their AI work using Projects and
upload files for brand consistency.

**Format**: Guided Hands-On Workshop

**Part 1: What are Projects?**

-   Think of Projects as your digital workbench.

-   Each project holds a goal, instructions, chat history, and files.

-   It keeps your work organized and lets AI assist you with context.

**Part 2: Creating a Project**

-   Name your Project: \"\[Name\] Brand Website\"

-   Add a Note: \"This project will help me use AI to build a
    personal/brand website.\"

-   Upload file: **Branding Guideline** (example provided)

**Part 3: Explaining the Branding File**

-   Use simple language: \"This file tells AI about your colors, fonts,
    and tone.\"

-   Show examples of what the guide includes (ManateeIT brand guideline)

**Part 4: Add Persona Worksheet**

-   Upload participant persona sheets (completed during workshop)

-   These describe tone of voice, target audience, and personality

**Part 5: Start Prompting** Participants use prompts like:

-   \"Using my brand guide, write a homepage headline.\"

-   \"Using the persona file, create an About section.\"

-   \"Give me 5 CTAs that match the brand tone.\"

**Part 6: Workshop Flow**

-   Instructors circulate to help refine prompts and explain referencing
    files

-   Encourage experimenting with rewriting content, tone, and format

**Outcome**: Participants have one ChatGPT Project set up with uploaded
branding and persona files, and are generating tailored website content.

**Provided Workshop Files**:

1.  Branding Guideline (e.g. ManateeIT sample)

2.  Persona Worksheet (fillable PDF or printed)

3.  Website Outline Template

4.  Prompt Starter Sheet (headline, bio, CTA, tagline examples)

Next sessions will build from this foundation into visual layout
planning and full site building using Lovable.

**🔷 Session 3 (11:00 AM -- 12:30 PM): Website Strategy & Design
Thinking**

**Objective**: Guide participants to structure their website using their
AI-generated content and begin visual planning.

**Format**: Collaborative Planning & Sketching

**Activities**:

-   **Lecture: The Anatomy of a Website (10 mins)**

    -   Homepage, About, Services, Contact, CTA sections

    -   Menu layout, scroll depth, sections vs pages

-   **Exercise: Define Your Website Goal (10 mins)**

    -   What do you want your visitor to do? (Buy, Book, Contact, Learn)

-   **Worksheet: Wireframe Your Site (20 mins)**

    -   Participants sketch a layout: header, image placement, sections,
        footer

-   **Group Share: Review & Feedback (20 mins)**

    -   Partner feedback or mini group roundtables

-   **Apply AI Content (30 mins)**

    -   Match the ChatGPT output to their layout

    -   Revise and request missing sections if needed

**Outcome**: Participants have a clear website layout with personalized
content matched to it, ready to build in Lovable.

**🔷 Session 4 (1:30 PM -- 2:30 PM): Intro to Lovable -- No-Code Site
Builder**

**Objective**: Give a walkthrough of Lovable and help participants
recreate their wireframe in the visual editor.

**Format**: Live Demo + Hands-On Build

**Activities**:

-   **Live Demo (15 mins)**

    -   Show layout tools, drag-and-drop features, design settings

-   **Build Time (45 mins)**

    -   Participants recreate their homepage structure

    -   Add content from ChatGPT (headline, About, CTA)

    -   Instructors circulate to assist

**Outcome**: Each participant has the homepage structure of their site
created inside Lovable.

**🔷 Session 5 (2:30 PM -- 4:00 PM): Build Sprint -- Finalizing with
ChatGPT & Lovable**

**Objective**: Support users in expanding their site with real content
and polishing design using AI.

**Format**: Guided Work Session

**Activities**:

-   **Enhance Content (30 mins)**

    -   Use ChatGPT to write bios, services, FAQ, testimonials

    -   Prompt tips for refining tone, length, and structure

-   **Add Visuals (30 mins)**

    -   Upload logos and brand assets

    -   Optional: Generate images with DALL·E or use royalty-free
        sources

-   **Style & Polish (30 mins)**

    -   Adjust fonts, spacing, buttons, links

    -   Make sure content matches brand guide tone and visual layout

**Outcome**: A functional site with complete content and custom design
ready for feedback.

**🔷 Session 6 (4:15 PM -- 5:00 PM): Show & Tell + Mini Launch Party**

**Objective**: Celebrate the work, share results, and reflect on what
was learned.

**Format**: Group Presentation & Feedback

**Activities**:

-   **5-Minute Showcase per Participant (30 mins)**

    -   Present site, share challenges and learnings

-   **Vote on Fun Awards (10 mins)**

    -   Most Creative, Best Use of AI, Cleanest Design

-   **Wrap-Up & Certs (5 mins)**

    -   Hand out digital or printed certificates

    -   Encourage next steps and continued learning

**Outcome**: Participants leave with a finished (or near-finished)
website and confidence to continue using AI.
