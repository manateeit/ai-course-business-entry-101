**🔧 Talking Points by Session**

**Session 1: Intro to AI (Lead)**

-   "AI is your assistant, not your replacement."

-   Use the calculator analogy.

-   Invite short responses: "Where have you seen AI used in your life?"

**Session 1.5: ChatGPT Setup (Tech Lead)**

-   Ensure everyone is logged in

-   Highlight Projects tab if Pro users are present

**Session 2: Mastering ChatGPT Projects**

-   "Think of Projects as a digital workspace."

-   Help participants upload their Brand Guide + Persona Worksheet

-   Encourage iterating prompts: "Let's try that again with a clearer
    tone."

**Session 3: Website Strategy & Design Thinking**

-   Walk through homepage anatomy: Hero, About, Services, CTA

-   Encourage sketching with the wireframe sheet

-   Partner share = great energy boost

**Session 4: Lovable Walkthrough**

-   "Lovable is your visual canvas --- just like Lego for websites."

-   Explain drag-and-drop block layout and preview

**Session 5: Build Sprint**

-   Help refine headlines, bios, service descriptions

-   Support image uploads or generating visual assets

**Session 6: Show & Tell + Mini Launch**

-   Keep it positive and celebratory

-   Award cards: "Most Creative," "Best Use of AI," etc.

-   Wrap up with: "What's one thing you'll keep using AI for after
    today?"
