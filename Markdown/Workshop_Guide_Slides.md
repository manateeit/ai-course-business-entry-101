# Session 1: Intro to AI: Light, Fun & Human

**Objective**: Introduce AI in simple, non-technical terms and explain its role in the day ahead.

**Activities**:
- **Warm Welcome**: Icebreaker prompt - "What would you use AI for today if you had a magic assistant?"
- **What is AI?** -- Calculator analogy, simple breakdown of machine learning and generative AI.
- **Who's Who**: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Meta (LLaMA)
- **Use Cases**: 5-minute brainstorm: "Where do you see AI in the wild?"
- **Busting Myths**: AI isn't here to replace you; it's here to assist you.

**Outcome**: Understand what AI is and how it will help throughout the day.

# Session 1.5: ChatGPT Setup

**Objective**: Ensure everyone is logged in and ready to work inside ChatGPT.

**Activities**:
- Create ChatGPT account (free or Pro)
- Walkthrough of ChatGPT interface
- Introduce "Projects" tab (for Pro users)

**Outcome**: All participants are logged into ChatGPT and ready to build.

# Session 2: Mastering ChatGPT Projects for Website Creation

**Objective**: Teach users to organize their AI work using Projects and upload files for brand consistency.

**Part 1: What are Projects?**
- Think of Projects as your digital workbench.
- Each project holds a goal, instructions, chat history, and files.
- It keeps your work organized and lets AI assist you with context.

**Part 2: Creating a Project**
- Name your Project: "[Name] Brand Website"
- Add a Note: "This project will help me use AI to build a personal/brand website."
- Upload file: **Branding Guideline** (example provided)

# Session 2 (continued)

**Part 3: Explaining the Branding File**
- Use simple language: "This file tells AI about your colors, fonts, and tone."
- Show examples of what the guide includes (ManateeIT brand guideline)

**Part 4: Add Persona Worksheet**
- Upload participant persona sheets (completed during workshop)
- These describe tone of voice, target audience, and personality

**Part 5: Start Prompting**
- "Using my brand guide, write a homepage headline."
- "Using the persona file, create an About section."
- "Give me 5 CTAs that match the brand tone."

# Session 3: Website Strategy & Design Thinking

**Objective**: Guide participants to structure their website using their AI-generated content and begin visual planning.

**Activities**:
- **Lecture: The Anatomy of a Website (10 mins)**
  - Homepage, About, Services, Contact, CTA sections
  - Menu layout, scroll depth, sections vs pages
- **Exercise: Define Your Website Goal (10 mins)**
  - What do you want your visitor to do? (Buy, Book, Contact, Learn)
- **Worksheet: Wireframe Your Site (20 mins)**
  - Participants sketch a layout: header, image placement, sections, footer
- **Group Share: Review & Feedback (20 mins)**
  - Partner feedback or mini group roundtables

# Session 4: Intro to Lovable -- No-Code Site Builder

**Objective**: Give a walkthrough of Lovable and help participants recreate their wireframe in the visual editor.

**Activities**:
- **Live Demo (15 mins)**
  - Show layout tools, drag-and-drop features, design settings
- **Build Time (45 mins)**
  - Participants recreate their homepage structure
  - Add content from ChatGPT (headline, About, CTA)
  - Instructors circulate to assist

**Outcome**: Each participant has the homepage structure of their site created inside Lovable.

# Session 5: Build Sprint -- Finalizing with ChatGPT & Lovable

**Objective**: Support users in expanding their site with real content and polishing design using AI.

**Activities**:
- **Enhance Content (30 mins)**
  - Use ChatGPT to write bios, services, FAQ, testimonials
  - Prompt tips for refining tone, length, and structure
- **Add Visuals (30 mins)**
  - Upload logos and brand assets
  - Optional: Generate images with DALL·E or use royalty-free sources
- **Style & Polish (30 mins)**
  - Adjust fonts, spacing, buttons, links
  - Make sure content matches brand guide tone and visual layout

# Session 6: Show & Tell + Mini Launch Party

**Objective**: Celebrate the work, share results, and reflect on what was learned.

**Activities**:
- **5-Minute Showcase per Participant (30 mins)**
  - Present site, share challenges and learnings
- **Vote on Fun Awards (10 mins)**
  - Most Creative, Best Use of AI, Cleanest Design
- **Wrap-Up & Certs (5 mins)**
  - Hand out digital or printed certificates
  - Encourage next steps and continued learning

**Outcome**: Participants leave with a finished (or near-finished) website and confidence to continue using AI.
