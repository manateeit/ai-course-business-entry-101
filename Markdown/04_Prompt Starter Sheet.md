**✨ Using ChatGPT to Generate Website Content**

Use these prompt starters to quickly begin creating content for your
website. Customize them with your brand info or uploaded files like your
Brand Guideline and Persona Worksheet.

**📍 Homepage**

**Headline / Tagline:**

\"Write a homepage headline that communicates \[what I do\] in a
\[tone\] voice using my brand guide.\"

**Intro / Welcome Message:**

\"Write a welcoming intro paragraph for my homepage that introduces me
and what I offer. Use the tone from my persona worksheet.\"

**Call-To-Action (CTA):**

\"Give me 5 call-to-action lines that encourage visitors to \[contact,
book, explore, etc.\]. Keep them short and branded.\"

**👨‍🎓 About Page**

**About Section:**

\"Write a professional yet friendly \'About Me\' paragraph using my
persona worksheet and mission statement.\"

**Brand Story:**

\"Create a short brand origin story based on these key points: \[bullet
list of history or vision\].\"

**🏢 Services or Products**

**Service Descriptions:**

\"Create a short, benefit-driven description for the following services:
\[list your services\]. Make them customer-focused.\"

**Product Summaries:**

\"Write 3-sentence summaries of each of my top 3 products. Use friendly
and engaging language.\"

**✉️ Contact Page**

**Contact Invitation:**

\"Write a simple, warm paragraph inviting users to reach out. Make it
sound personal and on-brand.\"

**Alternative CTAs:**

\"Suggest 3 creative ways to encourage visitors to take action besides
\'Contact Us.\'\"

**🌟 Bonus Prompts**

**Testimonials:**

\"Write a sample testimonial from a happy client who loved \[your
service\]. Keep it realistic and positive.\"

**FAQ:**

\"Based on my business, generate 3 common customer questions and concise
answers.\"

**Slogan Generator:**

\"Create 5 brand slogans that reflect \[my brand personality
keywords\].\"

**Tips for Prompting:**

-   Be specific about what you want (length, tone, format)

-   Reference your uploaded files

-   Don't be afraid to revise and re-prompt

**Pro Tip:** Add this sheet to your ChatGPT Project Notes so you can
